# Customer Success Story: Attributary + Parallax Media

## The Challenge

Parallax Media, a London-based performance agency, managed paid media for 45 clients across DTC consumer, B2B SaaS and hospitality. Every monthly client report was assembled from scratch: analysts spent two to three days per client stitching platform exports into Looker Studio dashboards with bespoke branding. For Parallax's largest client, Verana Apparel, a D2C women's fashion brand spending around $800k/month on paid, the pressure was sharper still. Verana's CMO had been asking for nine months for incrementality evidence on her TV and influencer budgets, and Parallax's existing stack could not produce it.

**45** — client workspaces live in 90 days
**$2.4M** — Verana annual spend reallocated on MMM findings
**4pp** — agency gross margin uplift across the retained book

## The Solution

Parallax rolled out Attributary Core across its whole book as a multi-tenant deployment: one workspace per client, over 200 connectors in total, client-branded reports auto-refreshed daily. Core's AI reasoning layer now flags anomalies at the client level and routes them straight to the responsible account manager. For Verana specifically, Parallax stood up a dedicated workspace with both Core and Measurement. MMM runs monthly across paid, linear TV, influencer and organic. The first incrementality test, on Verana's linear TV flights, produced the number Verana's CMO had been asking for.

> "Measurement turned Verana from a high-risk renewal into a three-year commit. Core turned our margins around across the rest of the book: the analysts who used to build reports now run strategy."
> — Tomás Reyes, Founding Partner, Parallax Media

## The Results

Within Year 1, Verana reallocated $2.4M of annual spend out of underperforming linear TV flights into digital and experiential, validated by in-market incrementality tests. Across the broader Parallax book, where most clients spend between $15k and $80k/month on paid and sit well below the Measurement threshold, analyst hours saved on reporting lifted agency gross margin by four percentage points. Parallax renewed every Attributary client at year-end, and Verana expanded into a three-year enterprise agreement covering Core and Measurement across its UK and US operations.

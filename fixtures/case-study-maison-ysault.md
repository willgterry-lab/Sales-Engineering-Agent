# Customer Success Story: Attributary + Maison Ysault

## The Challenge

Maison Ysault, a 140-year-old Parisian leather goods and ready-to-wear house within a global luxury holding group, ran six regional marketing organisations on six different reporting stacks. Group CMO Élise Vidal could not produce a single coherent quarterly view of paid performance across Europe, North America and APAC. With roughly $24M of annual paid spend, a minority of total marketing investment, with the larger share in experiential, editorial placement and celebrity dressing, the group CFO was sceptical that digital attribution told the real story. Measurement discipline across non-digital channels was effectively absent.

**$8M** — annual spend reallocated on Year-1 MMM output
**2.4x** — ROAS uplift on experiential after incrementality-driven restructuring
**6 regions** — unified on a single marketing data foundation

## The Solution

Maison Ysault selected Attributary Core as the group marketing data foundation, feeding into the existing Snowflake and Databricks environment. Measurement was stood up in parallel, with MMM refreshed quarterly at the group level and monthly at the regional level, alongside incrementality testing on experiential and influencer campaigns, the exact channels the CFO had been most sceptical of. Core's AI reasoning layer runs on creative-level anomalies across regions, catching performance drift within two days rather than at the next month-end cycle. Implementation ran as a joint programme between Attributary's solutions team and Ysault's Group Marketing Technology function over an 18-week window.

> "We plan the year on data we trust now, which is not a claim I could have made twelve months ago. The conversation with our CFO has completely changed."
> — Élise Vidal, Group CMO, Maison Ysault

## The Results

Year-one MMM output reallocated $8M of annual spend across channels. The most material finding was a re-weighting of experiential and influencer investment after incrementality testing showed each category had been under-credited by last-touch digital attribution by a factor of roughly two. The group-wide data foundation became the default reference for quarterly business reviews at the holding-group level. Attributary expanded into a multi-year enterprise commitment at the end of Year 1, covering all six regions and extending Measurement into the group's two sister maisons.

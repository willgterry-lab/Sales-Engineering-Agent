# Customer Success Story: Attributary + Aurelia Skin

## The Challenge

Aurelia Skin, a fast-growing DTC skincare brand, had scaled from a single hero SKU to a 40-product range in under two years. The analytics function hadn't kept pace. A three-person marketing team spent most of Monday morning stitching together exports from Meta, TikTok, Google Ads, Klaviyo and Shopify into a shared Google Sheet. By the time the weekly review happened on Tuesday, the numbers were already stale, and iOS attribution gaps on TikTok meant the blended ROAS figure nobody entirely trusted.

**9 HOURS** — weekly reporting time reclaimed (from 12 to 3)
**3 HOURS** — end-to-end data freshness (from 48)
**11 SOURCES** — unified in one warehouse

## The Solution

Aurelia onboarded onto Attributary Core across 11 connectors spanning paid social, search, email, organic and Shopify. Daily refreshes pipe into a BigQuery warehouse the team's solo BI analyst had set up six months earlier but could never reliably populate. Core's AI reasoning layer flags anomalies in creative spend. For example, surfacing a 2.1x CPM spike on a single TikTok ad set two days before the weekly review would have caught it. Attributary's solutions team recommended holding off on Measurement until Aurelia's paid spend crosses the $200k/month threshold where model-based measurement earns its keep.

> "We got our Mondays back. More importantly, we got numbers we actually trust. I can show my co-founder a spend chart and know the TikTok figure isn't lying to us."
> — Lena Okafor, Head of Marketing, Aurelia Skin

## The Results

Three months post-launch, Aurelia redeployed the reclaimed Monday morning into weekly creative testing, now running eight concurrent Meta and TikTok creative variants instead of two. In month two, an anomaly flagged by Core's reasoning layer surfaced a reporting error on the Google Ads side that had been inflating blended ROAS by 18% for six weeks. Aurelia preferred to catch it early. The wider Attributary roadmap conversation, including a future Measurement engagement once paid spend scales past $200k/month, is now a planned Year 2 step, not a pitch.
